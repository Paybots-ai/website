What Paybots does for you

Checks every purchase against what you asked for

You told the agent to book a flight under $400. Paybots checks the purchase against that intent, not just your card balance. If the charge doesn't match what you asked, it doesn't go through.

Rules you set, agents respect

Spend caps, categories you're okay with, categories you're not, approval thresholds for bigger purchases. Set them once. Your agent operates inside them.

Backed by Paybots

If the agent buys the wrong thing, picks the wrong merchant, or gets tricked by a prompt injection attack, that's not your problem. Purchases that clear Paybots are backed by Paybots.

How it works

1. Confirm.

At first, every purchase your agent wants to make pings you for a quick yes-or-no. Nothing happens without your tap.

2. Set the rules.

Once you've seen the agent get a few right, you set the boundaries: limits, merchants, categories. The agent works inside them.

3. Let it run.

Over time, as Paybots learns what you want, the boundaries loosen. Eventually the agent can act on intent alone, on purchases Paybots backs end to end.

  • Order the diapers when stock runs low.

  • Pay back a friend without opening an app.
  • Refill the office supplies without thinking about it.

The agent handles it. You stay in control.
